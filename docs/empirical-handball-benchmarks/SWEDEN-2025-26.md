## Comparisons between leagues

Describe environments without causal explanations. Herrallsvenskan, Handbollsligan Herr, Handbollsligan Dam and Damallsvenskan keep distinct competition/category identities. Goals and rule frequency differ by source population; differences do not establish causes. HBL match-level keeper data and women’s keeper-season data cannot be exchanged as equivalent observations.

## Denominators and transforms

Total goals = home + away official full-time result. Team goals use two team-match observations per match. Scorer concentration = highest player goals / team goals after goal-sum reconciliation. 7m attempts = explicit made + missed attempts, never awards; conversion = made / attempts. Suspensions count two-minute penalties, with protocol minute durations divided by two and PDF 2MIN counts retained.

HBL PDF ordinary and 7m columns are separate. Keeper saves and shots faced explicitly add both; require shotsFaced = saves + recorded goalsConceded. Conditional 7m save rate uses saves / (saves + made 7m goals), excluding wide/post attempts. Recorded field-player attempts exclude keeper shooting; outfieldConversion uses only field-player goals/attempts. The sourceTechnicalErrors series sums recorded field-player pass errors + lost balls + rule errors; keeper error columns are not normalized in v1. This is not an all-player turnover benchmark or the canonical ledger’s narrower error series.

Team profiles contain historical goals-for/goals-against distributions and descriptive competition deviations. Player-season profiles use actual official statistics, not gameplay attributes or CA/PA. No empirical ability coefficient is fitted.
