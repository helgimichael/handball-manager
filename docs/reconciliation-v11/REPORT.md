## 4. Low-volume root cause

Containment could leave stale carrier/entry relationships and a false finishing phase. A named back could keep an obsolete run or RECEIVE action; a genuinely prepared stationary return then fell back to the legacy selector. The attack lost opportunities through repeated passes and failed continuations. The repair addresses this lifecycle, not the score. See CAUSAL-NOTES.md.

## 12. Suspension underproduction root cause

The full duel-beaten margin also served as the illegal-hold gate, excluding genuine partial shoulder loss. A separate contact read now recognizes that lost position; full beaten recognition and defensive allocation remain independent. Development also exposed excessive holds at small losses, so the final technique choice grades the actual loss and discipline.

## 13. Legal side containment

A defender retaining the shoulder uses bent arms. Legal lateral torso containment and legitimately recovered position do not create automatic holds or suspensions. Light/stationary rear touches do not inherit the intense-restraint category. Deterministic scenarios pass.

## 14. Illegal lost-shoulder hold

An executing penetration with actual partial shoulder loss can produce an illegal hold before the full duel-beaten margin. The choice grades real lost position and discipline; the existing referee assesses the resulting contact. The controlled lost-shoulder case produces the appropriate foul and progressive suspension.
