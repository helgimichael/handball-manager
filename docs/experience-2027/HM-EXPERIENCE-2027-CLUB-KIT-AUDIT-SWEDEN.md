> Generated by `club-identity/build-registry.mjs` (2026-11-13). All **105 Swedish clubs** in the game database (Handbollsligan, Allsvenskan, Division 1, Division 2). Phase 2 standard (owner): VERIFIED = official match photo or official source where the men's A-team home shirt is identifiable and the colour can be judged with high confidence; a shop product image alone is never enough. PROBABLE = clear visual evidence short of that (older photos, unconfirmed season, press). UNCERTAIN = conflicting / several possible shirts / unclear team or kit. UNKNOWN = no reliable evidence. **No colour is guessed.**
> "Measured" = the original HEX was measured from the evidence photo (median of plain shirt fabric, `club-identity/sample_colour.py`); otherwise the documented colour name maps to the shared palette. Crests keep their own colours.
