# HM Experience 2027 — Complete Club Kit Colour Audit

> Generated by `design-lab/experience-2027/club-identity/build-registry.mjs` (2026-11-13). Design principle (owner, 2026-11-13): **every club's neon identity originates from the dominant colour of its actual first-choice home shirt.**
> Scope: **all 202 clubs** in the game database (read-only extraction from `apps/game/lib/game-data/clubs.ts` and the Division 2 registrations in `lib/competition/transition/sweden2026Data.json`). No game data was changed. The game's current `primaryColor` values are documented in source as unverified crest placeholders and were **not** used as evidence.

## Method

1. **Evidence.** Researchers looked for text that states the current home-shirt colour, in this priority order: official club shop listings, official club or social posts, kit manufacturers, match reports, Wikipedia kit templates. They read pages as text and **did not measure colours in photographs**; they recorded documented colour names and any documented exact hex.
2. **Colour value.** A documented hex is used as-is (`hexSource: documented`). Otherwise the documented colour *name* maps to one shared standard palette (`hexSource: standard-palette`), so the same name always gives the same value:

`white` #FFFFFF · `black` #111111 · `grey` #8A8D91 · `silver` #A7A9AC · `red` #C8102E · `dark-red` #9B1B30 · `wine` #722F37 · `maroon` #6D2333 · `orange` #F57C00 · `yellow` #FFD100 · `gold` #C99A2E · `green` #00843D · `dark-green` #0B5D3B · `light-green` #6CC24A · `blue` #0057B8 · `royal-blue` #1F4FB4 · `light-blue` #5BB5E8 · `sky-blue` #4FA3DC · `navy` #14284B · `turquoise` #00A3AD · `teal` #007C80 · `purple` #5B2C8F · `pink` #E4007C

3. **Neon** (`club-identity/neon.js`, shared by the lab and this build):
   - Source colour: the chromatic home-shirt colour; for white, black or grey shirts, the documented secondary, then the trim colour.
   - Conversion: OKLCH, keeping the hue, with fixed lightness and chroma targets per hue family (yellows lighter so they stay yellow), clipped to the sRGB gamut.
   - Accessibility gate: neon on the Arena night background `#070b12` and dark ink `#061018` on neon must both be at least 4.5:1.
   - Shirts that are white, black or grey with no documented accent get **monochrome ice-silver** `#d7e1ee`. No hue is invented.
   - Unknown or unverified kits get **pending steel** `#9fb0c6`. A clubless manager uses **neon orange** `#ff7a1a` (owner decision).
4. **Overrides** live only in `club-identity/overrides.json`, with a reason, owner and date. None are applied unless listed there.
