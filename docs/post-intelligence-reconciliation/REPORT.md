## Implemented behavior retained

Defenders regain availability from actual goal-side containment, with lateral distance and the defended shoulder retained in the relationship. A beaten body's resistance is reduced rather than erased. Screen/wrong-foot debt remains distinct. The connected defensive line responds to observed loss of containment; lateral order remains covered by regression tests.

An assigned moving defender starts real contact preparation after reaching the carrier. Oblique side containment no longer defaults to a deliberate hold; illegal late holds require genuinely lost shoulder containment and the defender's discipline. A physically reachable original beaten defender can still attempt a late hold after responsibility hands over. Contact releases after a genuine pass changes the carrier. Nearby back-to-goal pivot marking remains pressure even when another defender owns carrier pressure.

Legal helper prevention considers reachable hands/body rather than requiring the defender to occupy the attacker's future body centre. Close-shot selection and pressure include actual containment. A sufficiently strong finisher still has a contested shot option. Goalkeeper reaction limits moving hands without erasing the torso already intersecting the ball. A curved-area loose ball has a legal collection approach without snapping the ball or awarding ownership.

New production matches identify behavior as `career-post-intelligence-reconciled-v1`.
