## What was built

### C5.1 Shell (ENT-07 C, ENT-07a A, ENT-08 C, ENT-08a A, ENT-10 C)

**Six hubs** (`lib/career/hubs.ts`) over the unchanged 27 destinations:

| Hub | Destinations |
|---|---|
| Idag | Hem, Inkorg, Kalender, Schema |
| Laget | Trupp, Taktik, Träning, Medicinskt, Kontrakt |
| Klubben | Klubbhuset, Klubb, Styrelsen, Ekonomi, Personal |
| Rekrytering | Rekrytering, Spelarsök, Scouting, Transfers |
| Tävling | Säsongen, Tävlingar, Svenska Cupen, Statistik, Analys |
| Karriär | Karriär, Historik, Media |

- Inställningar stays at the foot of the rail.
- Every legacy URL resolves to its hub and tab: player profiles stay under Laget, match pages under Idag › Schema.
- Keys 1–6 jump between hubs. They are ignored while typing, while the palette is open and during a live match.

**Rail:**

- 220 px pinned from 1360 px wide; collapsed to 64 px below that.
- Expands on hover or focus as an overlay; a toggle in the foot is remembered per device.

**Hub header:** a breadcrumb (Kontoret › hub › tab) and a tab row for the hub.

**Week Spine** (`lib/career/weekSpine.ts`, `components/shell/WeekSpine.tsx`). A pure Monday–Sunday model built from real state only:

- own fixtures and results;
- training day types and sessions;
- unresolved decisions;
- open press conferences and board meetings;
- transfer window open and close;
- agreed transfers;
- expiring contracts;
- scouting completions;
- league start.

It also provides:

- a matchday clock that counts down to the next match and lights up on matchday;
- a Continue stop marker on the next own match, or on today while decisions are pending;
- an "Härnäst" queue with the next three items (ENT-10).

On Idag it expands, with a selectable day detail. It is hidden on the live-match route.

**Command palette** (`lib/career/commandPalette.ts`, `components/shell/CommandPalette.tsx`):

- Opens with Ctrl/Cmd+K or the header field.
- Groups: Sidor, Åtgärder (navigation only — the palette never changes the career), Spelare, Klubbar, Tävlingar.
- Prefixes `@` `#` `>`; recent items; arrows, Enter, Tab to cycle groups, Escape.
- A full-screen sheet on phones.

**Phone dock:**

- A docked full-width Continue above bottom tabs: Idag · Laget · Tävling · Marknad · Mer.
- Mer opens the full hub map with the save controls.
- The alpha feedback button is lifted above the dock.

**Top-bar overflow (ENT-08 C):** quick Spara stays visible; Spara och avsluta, export and import move into a ⋯ menu.

### C5.2 Matchweek Front Page (DSH-01 C, Arena adaptation)

Built by `lib/career/frontPage.ts` and `app/career/page.tsx`:

- **Masthead:** a broadcast strap with the club nameplate and the ISO week, plus two ring "ears":
  - board confidence, with change and top factor;
  - manager reputation, with the last change and its reason.
- **Lead story,** chosen from state. Priority order:
  1. live match
  2. season complete
  3. unemployed
  4. matchday
  5. result within two days
  6. board warning
  7. transfer deadline
  8. injury crisis
  9. next-match preview
- **"Att avgöra":** numbered decisions (open events, press, expiring contracts, board offer), each with an inline action.
- **Ladder ±2** and **Runt ligan** / **Från klubben** briefs.

The existing match hero, availability, weekly overview and board card remain as depth below.

Verified live: matchday lead, then the "Matchen pågår" lead during the match, then a "Förlust · Cup — V.Irsta 29–26 IFK Tumba" lead linking to the match report.

### C5.3 Squad Matrix (SQD-01 A) + Peek

Built by `lib/career/squadMatrix.ts`, `app/career/squad/page.tsx`, `components/shell/PlayerChip.tsx` and `PlayerPeek.tsx`.

**Player chip:** shirt number, name (profile link), position, energy bar, match-fitness band pip, morale dot, form sparkline, and K / VK / 7m pills.

**Saved views:** four, of 8 columns each (Detailed density up to 12):

- Matchberedskap
- Prestation
- Kontraktsrisk
- Utveckling

**Table:**

- Fits 1280 px with no horizontal scroll.
- Sticky header and sticky chip column.
- Every metric is sortable, including Skott % and Räddn %; missing values always sort last.

**Control panel:** views, search, position segments plus a select, state, form mode and density.

**Selection tray** (`applyTrayAction`, existing write paths only):

- compare two players;
- bulk squad role;
- rest or full training load;
- captain or vice (exactly one player);
- 7 m via the tactics page;
- transfer list (asks for confirmation, warns about morale).

Players the tray could not act on are listed with the reason.

**Peek:** a right-hand drawer (a bottom sheet on phones) with "Öppna hela sidan".

**Phone:** ranked cards showing the current view's top three metrics.

### C5.4 Week Spine Calendar (WLD-05 C)

- Three views: Vecka (current week expanded), Månad (four weeks) and Lista (goes to Schema).
- A season strip shows every week:
  - own results as win/draw/loss;
  - upcoming matches;
  - transfer windows;
  - national-team breaks;
  - the current week.

  Clicking a week jumps to it.

## Blender, Higgsfield, CSS and SVG

**Blender and Higgsfield: evaluated again, not used.**

- Every new surface is data-dense and changes with career state.
- Rendered 3D or generated imagery would add download weight and could not show state.
- The existing environment photography carries atmosphere on the front-page lead and the squad banner.

**CSS and SVG: used.**

- The ring meters on the ears are inline SVG.
- The Spine pins, energy bars, pips and season strip are CSS.
- Motion is limited to the palette and Peek entrances (140–160 ms) and the rail expanding. All of it is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

There are no new dependencies, and no second design system: Arena tokens only, and the overrides generator stays up to date.
