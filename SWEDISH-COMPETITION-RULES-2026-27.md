# Swedish men's competition rules 2026/27

M1.2A research; checked 2026-11-07. Governs the season ending in spring 2027 and qualification for 2027/28. No results or future-season regulations are presumed.

## Decision

Use season-specific rank selectors, eligibility checks and connected qualification stages. The four leagues are SHF-administered federal competitions; Division 2 and below are district-administered (TB §2:1). There is no uniform two-up/two-down rule. [TB chapter 2][TB2]

## Match and ranking rules

Regular matches are 2×30 minutes (Rule 2:1). A required winner uses two successive 2×5-minute extra-time periods if necessary (Rule 2:2). [Official rulebook][RULE2]

Points: win/draw/loss = 2/1/0. Ranking: points, goal difference, goals scored, mutual results; a remaining tie affecting promotion/relegation requires a neutral deciding match. Odd-length series finish when one team has an unassailable wins lead. These are competition rules, not permission to substitute a random lot. [TB §2:7][TB2]

Two-leg qualification uses match points, then combined goal difference; equal points/difference require a decider after leg two, without away-goals priority. Best-of-three/five games cannot finish drawn. Cross-series vacancy comparisons use points/played, then goal-difference/played. [THB §§2:9, 2:13][THB2]

SM playoffs: QF 1–8, 4–5, 2–7, 3–6; first two winners feed SF1, the others SF2, then final. All best-of-five, H-B-H-B-H for the better regular-season finisher; champion and league winner are separate honours. [THB §§2:1-3][THB2]

## Eligibility and exceptions

- Declined/ineligible advancement passes to the next eligible finisher, only through the upper half (middle place included for an odd-sized league). Exhaustion leads to vacancy handling or cancellation. Qualification uses the destination tier's regulations; elite-licence refusal prevents HBL qualification. [TB §2:3][TB2]
- Cooperation associations are distinct identities. An active B association cannot qualify to A's current division; only A associations can occupy the top two tiers. Ending an agreement by 31 December affects advancement rights. If A's relegation brings both to one tier, the agreement dissolves; retaining it requires B to give up advancement or be relegated. Use §§9:5-6 rather than guessing from similar names. [TB §§9:1, 9:4-7][TB9]
- Ordinary reserve teams and cooperation associations are different categories; reserve placement restrictions have their own hierarchy and forced-relegation dependencies. [TB §2:5][TB2]
- HBL licensing appeals can expand membership beyond 14. Vacancies are phase-dependent, with ordered offers. Geographic allocation is national/travel-based, with TK discretion. If qualification is cancelled, only direct promotion/relegation remains. [THB3 §3:2 pp.11-12; §3:5 pp.14-17; chapter introduction p.8][THB3]

For implementation, keep explicit administrative decisions for licensing, withdrawals, appeals, replacements, cancellation and group allocation. An unimplemented exception must produce a pending decision, not an invented winner or balancing club. Full vacancy orders remain at the cited sections; this report does not reduce them to a generic “best relegated club” fallback.
