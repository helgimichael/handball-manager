## Summary

M6.0 bounds the delegation and scouting state that grew without limit, guarantees that proposal and assignment ids are never reused, closes opponent-scouting work that can no longer be done, resets opponent analysis and routine press on a club change (with a clear notice), and fixes two scouting bugs:
- league scouting abroad now uses international knowledge;
- league pools follow promotion and relegation.

It needs **no save-schema change and no migration**. The only type change adds two values to the activity log's `result` union.

**Evidence:**
- All 12 acceptance criteria pass.
- The full suite shows exactly the 12 known baseline failures and nothing new.
- A 5-season career with every area delegated, rolled over and reloaded five times, keeps its delegation state flat: about 110–125 kB after each rollover, against 345–484 kB before it.

## Remaining limitations

1. **Total save growth outside M6.0** (about 0.9 MB gzip a season in this run). Candidates for a later save-size task, not M6: season history, world player pools and similar.
2. **`legacy-before-save.json`** stops at a CALENDAR_CONFLICT in the cup when played on. The conflict is in the save's own fixtures. M6.0 does not touch the calendar, so this is pre-existing. It was not investigated further, and a separate check on main is recommended if that save matters.
3. **Notices older than 400 days are pruned** by the existing inbox rule, so a long career does not keep the "Motståndaranalysen saknar ansvarig" notice forever. Intended.
4. **The legacy OPPONENT_ANALYSIS career task** still produces "Motståndaranalys klar" events next to the delegated analyst (G-B3). That is M6.1 scope and unchanged here.
5. **In DELEGATE mode, training plans with individual instructions** still wait for approval and expire if ignored. This is the existing M6C/M6D rule ("Individuella instruktioner kräver ditt godkännande"). M6.0 now logs those expiries, so training reaches its 40-entry cap.
6. **Scoped lint:** one pre-existing warning about an unused import in `delegation.ts`, left as it is (not M6.0 scope).
7. **Browser check** covered the responsibilities page on a fresh local QA career at `localhost:3751`, on desktop and at 375 px. The press notice and the analyst notice were verified by tests, not in the browser.
